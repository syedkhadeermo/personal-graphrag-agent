# Personal AI Research Knowledge

## Computational Drug Discovery

I am developing an AI-assisted computational drug discovery workflow.

The workflow includes molecular property analysis, ADME/Tox prediction,
protein-ligand docking, and molecular dynamics simulation.

AutoDock Vina is used for molecular docking.

GROMACS and AMBER are considered for molecular dynamics and validation.
